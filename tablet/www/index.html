Soro X

Tablet companion for Soro X on your computer

Your computer

    Restarted Soro X on your computer? Its pairing code changes: scan the new one below.

    Pair with your computer

    1. On your computer, open Soro X → Settings → Sync.
    2. Turn on Enable Phone Mirror and Allow LAN access.
    3. Make sure this tablet is on the same Wi-Fi, then scan the QR code.
